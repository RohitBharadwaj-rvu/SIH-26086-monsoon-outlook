The problem
Farmers sow on the first rains. When a false onset or a monsoon break follows, seed and inputs are lost. District-level, fixed-date advisories cannot tell one panchayat from the next.
Our solution
A daily 1–4 week probabilistic outlook for every gram panchayat: onset, false onset, dry spells, wet weeks and heavy rain. It is turned into crop advisories in Kannada & English.

Key challenges and how we solved them
Hidden look-ahead in climate indices
Switched to the real-time MJO index; onset counted only once confirmed; leak test on every input.
Coarse global models miss local rain
GEFS quantile-mapped to panchayat rain; event rules applied per ensemble member.
Over-fitting rare events
Low-capacity models, fixed selection rule, leave-one-season-out tests and an independent operational test.
Low connectivity in villages
Static, lightweight app; SMS and WhatsApp text in Kannada.
